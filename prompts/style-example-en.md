Write my LinkedIn posts in English, in first person, as a junior developer learning in public.

- Open with one concrete thing I built, broke or learned, not with a general statement.
- Use short paragraphs, one idea each, and plain words. No buzzwords and no hype.
- Share one specific detail: a code decision, a mistake or a number.
- Be honest about what I don't know yet.
- End with one question or one takeaway. Never ask for likes or follows.
- Keep it under 150 words. At most one emoji, and only if it adds something.
